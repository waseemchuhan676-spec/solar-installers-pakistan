Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

This website does not run accounts, logins, payments, reviews or tracking of its own. It sets no cookies.

What we collect

Nothing, directly. We do not ask you for personal information to browse the directory, and we do not store anything you type into the search box or the filters — that stays in your browser.

The Add a listing form

The form on the Add a listing page sends nothing to a server. When you submit it, it opens your own email app with a pre-filled message to waseemchuhan676@gmail.com. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app, and nothing you type is stored on this website. Only send business details you are happy to have published — the listing itself, once verified and published, is public.

Email

If you email us, we keep your message only long enough to answer it and to verify any listing or correction you sent. We do not sell, share or publish your email address.

Links to other sites

Each listing links to the company's own website, and the sources section links to public lists such as ppib.gov.pk. Those sites have their own privacy policies; this policy does not cover them.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google may log your IP address under Google's own privacy policy, as it does for any site that uses Google Fonts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: waseemchuhan676@gmail.com.
