About this directory

Solar Installers Pakistan is a free directory of verified solar installers across Pakistan — 43 companies in 8 cities: Karachi (8), Lahore (8), Islamabad (8), Rawalpindi (4), Faisalabad (4), Multan (4), Peshawar (4) and Quetta (3). It was built to answer one simple question: which solar companies in my city are real, and how do I reach them?

How a listing gets verified

Every phone number in this directory was taken from the company's own website, its official Facebook or business page, or the PPIB certified-installer list as transcribed by ProChuno — and checked on 9 November 2026. Nothing was taken from an unsourced blog list. Where a field could not be found in a public source, the card says not published — it was never guessed.

Companies with offices in several cities are listed once, under their head-office city, with their other offices noted on the card.

What "certified" means here — read this

The PPIB certified-installer PDFs directly available are from October 2024, and most of their validity dates have passed. Certificates marked via ProChuno come from that site's transcription of PPIB lists dated 29 October 2026, which could not be cross-checked against a live PPIB PDF. No installer on this site is presented as currently certified. A lapsed date does not prove a company lost its certification — firms renew — but always re-check the current list at ppib.gov.pk before paying an advance.

Prices

Only three companies in this directory publish system prices on their own websites: Alpha Solar, Beyond Green Solar and Solar Citizen. Their published figures are shown on the home page, exactly as published. Every other company quotes after a site survey, so for them the directory shows labelled market-guide ranges instead of a made-up price.

Net billing

Under NEPRA's Prosumer Regulations 2026, net billing replaced net metering for new applicants: exported units are credited at roughly Rs 11/unit instead of about Rs 26/unit before, and contracts run 5 years instead of 7. This directory does not promise the old net-metering terms — ask any installer to show you the current rules for your city before you sign.

Free, and no paid placement

Listings are free. There is no payment system, no login, and no review or rating system on this site. A listing's position in the directory is not sold — companies are listed by city, and the order within a city is the order they were verified.

Corrections

Details change — numbers move, offices move, certificates renew. If you spot something wrong, or a company that should be added, tell us. Corrections are checked against a public source before they go live.
