Net metering has changed — read before you sign

Under NEPRA’s Prosumer Regulations 2026 (SRO 251(I)/2026, 9 Feb 2026), net billing replaced net metering for new applicants: exported units are credited at roughly Rs 10–11 per unit (IESCO’s circular: Rs 8.13) instead of about Rs 26–27 under the old rules, and contracts run 5 years instead of 7. Agreements signed before Feb 2026 are grandfathered. An installer promising the old net-metering rates is quoting terms that no longer apply to new connections in Quetta (QESCO) — ask them to show you the current DISCO rules.
